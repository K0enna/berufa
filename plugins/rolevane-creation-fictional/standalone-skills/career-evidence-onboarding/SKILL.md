# Organize selected career evidence

Apply the manifest gate and sharing disclosure in the
[shared writing method](../../references/writing.md) before processing content.
Only `selected_text_standalone_v1` permits this workflow under the
[standalone boundary](../../references/standalone-profile.md). Require explicit agreement;
reuse it in this conversation until revoked or scope changes. Denial stops processing.

## Build an inspectable experience summary

Use deliberately pasted notes, excerpts and corrections. If asked to scan a drive, open a
CV or extract an attachment, explain the selected-text route and ask for the relevant paste
without reading that source. User labels identify supplied sources, not verified provenance.

1. Give each supplied excerpt a short working label, such as "Note A: 2024 review excerpt".
   Retain an exact supporting quotation or location within the paste for material claims.
   These labels are temporary associations, not canonical evidence IDs or approval receipts.
2. Separate responsibilities, individual actions, shared outcomes, skills, qualifications,
   scope and preferences. Keep dates, currency, periods, approximate metrics and collaborators.
   "Helped the team" cannot become sole ownership. Do not turn exposure into expertise.
3. Consolidate duplicates when they describe the same fact without dropping qualifiers or
   distinct time periods. For conflicting dates, titles, team sizes or metrics, show both
   versions and their source labels. Do not average them or silently choose one.
4. Distinguish a supported supplied claim, a transferable interpretation, an unsupported
   claim and an unresolved conflict. A claim's presence in notes is not independent verification.
   User corrections replace only the affected assertion; record the superseded wording.
5. Ask a small missing-information list aimed at useful claims: personal action, scope,
   result basis, dates, ownership or permitted public wording. Avoid requesting a full archive.

## Deliver usable material

Return an organized experience summary, then a compact claim/source table with exact
support, qualifiers and uncertainty. Include a conflict list and the questions that would
resolve it. Separate optional phrasing suggestions from factual claims. If requested, show
which claims can support a CV or interview story, with accurate bounded wording; do not
claim publication approval, validate credentials or certify truth.

No import, evidence approval or connected database is performed. No automatic persistence.
If the user requests a file, follow the selected-path Markdown/readback boundary. In later
sessions the user must supply the selected summary and corrections again with fresh agreement.

The [evidence example](../../examples/standalone-workflows.md#evidence) shows source associations
and a conflicting team-size note. Stop at the organized summary and requested phrasing.
