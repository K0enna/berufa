# Compare supplied options

Apply the manifest gate and sharing disclosure in the
[shared writing method](../../references/writing.md) before processing terms.
Only `selected_text_standalone_v1` permits this workflow under the
[standalone boundary](../../references/standalone-profile.md). Obtain explicit agreement;
reuse it in this conversation until revoked or scope changes. Denial stops processing.

## Make the comparison honest and useful

Use selected pasted option terms, a current baseline if supplied, and the user's priorities.
A hypothetical option is not a received offer. Source notes are supplied references, not
verified employer commitments. Preserve conditions and whether a value is an assumption.

1. Separate mandate, decision rights, reporting line, team, growth, learning and downside.
   A larger title with a smaller mandate is not automatically progression.
2. Normalize only compatible supplied terms. Keep currency, annual/monthly period, gross/net
   basis, fixed pay, target variable, guarantee, equity and benefits separate. Annualize a
   monthly amount only when the number of paid periods is supplied or clearly assumed and
   shown. Do not convert currencies, gross/net or contractual/tax terms without a valid basis.
3. Compare guaranteed cash independently from target cash. Add compatible fixed and target
   variable values to show target total, explicitly not expected or guaranteed income.
   Show a zero-variable scenario as an assumption, not a fact about likely payout. Use an
   expected payout only when the user supplies a justified probability/scenario and requests it.
4. Unknown amounts are unknown, never zero. Do not add equity face value to spendable cash,
   value a company car by guessing, or subtract unquantified travel costs. Retain vesting,
   liquidity, payout conditions and missing benefit valuations as open terms.
5. Compare travel, office days, relocation, working hours, stability and opportunity cost
   against constraints. Avoid company reputation or funding claims not supplied. Highlight
   where a practical burden could outweigh the known pay difference.
6. Show the arithmetic and assumptions beside each total/delta. Rank options only on the
   user's priorities and known terms; use a conditional recommendation if unknowns matter.
   Do not invent a weighted score or definitive total-economic winner from incomplete terms.

## Deliver the comparison

Return a side-by-side table of scope, fixed/variable pay, currency/period, benefits, burden and
unknowns; a short calculation section; material tradeoffs; questions to resolve terms; and
an appropriate next decision. With one option, compare it to the supplied baseline or state
that the baseline is unavailable. Still explain the option's known strengths and unknowns.

No contract signing, offer acceptance, negotiation message, employer contact or state change.
This prepares a career decision; it does not establish legal/tax terms. Requested local files
follow the new Markdown/readback boundary. Later sessions need selected terms and fresh agreement.

The [offer example](../../examples/standalone-workflows.md#offers) shows compatible annual
cash arithmetic while leaving unknown variable pay and benefits unresolved. Stop at comparison.
