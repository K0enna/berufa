# Prepare an interview round

Read the manifest gate, sharing disclosure and writing quality pass in the
[shared writing method](../../references/writing.md) before processing content.
Only `selected_text_standalone_v1` permits this workflow under the
[standalone boundary](../../references/standalone-profile.md). Require explicit agreement;
reuse it in this conversation until revoked or scope changes. Denial stops processing.

## Tailor preparation to the supplied basis

Use deliberately pasted role text, experience, stories and round details. If the round or
panel is unknown, prepare a general conversation and label that assumption. Do not invent
attendees, employer agendas or a scheduled interview. Preparation does not confirm attendance.

1. Identify the few role needs the conversation should test, plus the candidate's practical
   decision questions. Tailor emphasis to the supplied round: a recruiter discussion may
   focus on scope and constraints; a functional round needs deeper actions and tradeoffs.
2. Draft a concise speakable opening that connects current work, relevant supplied support
   and reason for considering this role. Do not invent motivation or praise the employer
   beyond what the user supplied. Keep uncertainty out of affirmative experience claims.
3. Select story material relevant to those needs. Separate situation, responsibility,
   personal action, collaborators and bounded outcome in preparation notes. If a story is
   sufficiently supplied, write a complete natural answer without reciting framework labels.
   Otherwise give a grounded answer start and precise prompts for the missing action/result;
   never fabricate a success to fill the story.
4. Prepare candid gap handling: what is absent, which supplied experience transfers, what
   would need learning, and what question tests the employer's flexibility. Do not claim
   exact domain experience from an adjacent product or an incomplete degree as completed.
5. Give employer questions tied to mandate, success measures, authority, interfaces and
   practical constraints. Mark unknown compensation or work terms rather than inventing them.
6. Apply the shared writing pass to spoken answers. Preserve shared outcomes and approximate
   metrics; make wording easy to say rather than polished beyond the candidate's actual voice.

## Deliver preparation the user can use

Return the opening, relevant story answers/prompts with their supplied basis, gap handling,
prioritized employer questions and a concise call sheet. The sheet should contain the
objective, opening cue, strongest evidence cues, key gap response and questions. Keep
logistics unknown unless supplied. Respect requests for a smaller output or critique-only;
do not add a full CV or application letter.

No live interview answers, call monitoring, recording, calendar changes or external messages.
No event is inferred or saved. Requested files follow the new-path Markdown/readback boundary.
A later session requires the selected brief and fresh sharing agreement.

The [interview example](../../examples/standalone-workflows.md#interview) shows an opening,
grounded story and practical call sheet. Stop at the requested preparation.
