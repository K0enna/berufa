# Prepare an interview round

Read the manifest gate, sharing disclosure and writing quality pass in the
[shared writing method](../../references/writing.md) before processing content.
Only `selected_text_standalone_v1` permits this workflow under the
[standalone boundary](../../references/standalone-profile.md). Require explicit agreement;
reuse it in this conversation until revoked or scope changes. Denial stops processing.

## Tailor preparation to the supplied basis

Use deliberately pasted role text, experience, stories and round details. If the round or
panel is unknown, prepare a general conversation and label that assumption. Do not invent
attendees, employer agendas or a scheduled interview. Preparation does not confirm attendance.

Reuse material already selected in this conversation. A scheduled interview, application or
pursue decision is not required for general practice. Distinguish supplied experience from
independently verified evidence. Do not research an employer or interviewer or open files.

Submitted CV and letter text are optional. Use the user's description to distinguish an exact
submitted version, an excerpt, a current draft and an uncertain version. Ask only if that status
changes the check; an unlabeled draft is not presumed submitted. State a missing or partial
document limit and continue role-based preparation. Never substitute a master CV or assume
which version the employer received.

## Prioritize questions you may be asked

When a preparation pack or likely questions are requested, provide at most six probes unless
the user chooses another scope. Rank preparation priority from supplied prior-round feedback,
material confirmed gaps, posting requirements and interview stage. Essential needs outrank
preferences; missing experience information is unknown, not a confirmed gap. Stage changes
the depth of a question without making a prediction certain.

For each probe show the question, priority, reason linked to a supplied source label/passage,
any relevant submitted claim, and story/evidence cues or missing support. Do not invent
probabilities, feedback or insider knowledge. Keep these candidate-facing questions separate
from questions to ask the employer. A mock-only request goes directly to practice instead
of forcing a full pack.

## Check the exact supplied submitted claims

Compare material claims with the selected facts, stories and user corrections. Retain each
document's source/version label and exact affected wording. Classify a claim as consistent
with the supplied basis, unsupported, ambiguous or contradicted; consistency is not verification.
Check personal versus team contribution, approximate versus exact results, team size and
qualification status. A submitted claim is not made true by being submitted.

Do not rehearse an unsupported or contradicted claim as fact. Ask one focused clarification
for the active conflict, while continuing unaffected preparation. A later correction updates
the answer basis without rewriting the historical submitted text or silently resolving other
conflicts. Accurate supplied details beyond a CV may be used when compatible with that basis.
Do not teach concealment or claim a whole-document check from an excerpt.

## Prepare the requested material

1. Identify the few role needs the conversation should test, plus the candidate's practical
   decision questions. Tailor emphasis to the supplied round: a recruiter discussion may
   focus on scope and constraints; a functional round needs deeper actions and tradeoffs.
2. Draft a concise speakable opening that connects current work, relevant supplied support
   and reason for considering this role. Do not invent motivation or praise the employer
   beyond what the user supplied. Keep uncertainty out of affirmative experience claims.
3. Select story material relevant to those needs. Separate situation, responsibility,
   personal action, collaborators and bounded outcome in preparation notes. If a story is
   sufficiently supplied, write a complete natural answer without reciting framework labels.
   Otherwise give a grounded answer start and precise prompts for the missing action/result;
   never fabricate a success to fill the story.
4. Prepare candid gap handling: what is absent, which supplied experience transfers, what
   would need learning, and what question tests the employer's flexibility. Do not claim
   exact domain experience from an adjacent product or an incomplete degree as completed.
5. Give employer questions tied to mandate, success measures, authority, interfaces and
   practical constraints. Mark unknown compensation or work terms rather than inventing them.
6. Apply the shared writing pass to spoken answers. Preserve shared outcomes and approximate
   metrics; make wording easy to say rather than polished beyond the candidate's actual voice.

## Practice interactively only when requested

Default to three primary questions unless the user sets another limit. State the small scope,
ask exactly one question and wait for the user's answer. Warm up when useful, then target the
relevant role needs and a difficult question. Follow-ups deepen the active question rather
than silently extending the number of primary questions. Do not invent a candidate response,
count a sample or hint as an attempt, or dump a scripted conversation.

After an actual answer, give one specific strength, one useful improvement and any factual
concern, all grounded in that answer and the supplied basis. If it lacks a supported strength,
say what is missing rather than invent praise. Do not infer spoken tone, confidence, hiring
readiness or a score from text. Ask at most one relevant follow-up or next question, then wait.
Apply the shared writing pass if the user requests revised spoken wording; preserve their facts.

- `hint`: give a cue for the active question without inventing a story or answering for the user.
- `retry`: repeat the active question and wait; do not advance or treat it as an answer.
- `skip`: mark the active question skipped and move to the next within the agreed scope.
- `stop`: stop asking and recap only actual attempts, useful corrections and unresolved support.

At the agreed limit, offer a concise recap instead of another question. Revocation or uncertain
sharing overrides feedback and recap: stop content processing without repeating supplied material.
Practice state stays in this conversation; do not save a transcript or claim durable progress.
If asked to answer during a real live interview, monitor, record, send or schedule, decline that
action and offer preparation for another time. Never infer an employer event from this practice.

## Deliver preparation the user can use

For a requested pack, return likely interviewer questions with their reasons and supplied basis,
the submitted-claim check when possible, opening, relevant story answers/prompts, gap handling,
prioritized employer questions and a concise call sheet. The sheet should contain the
objective, opening cue, strongest evidence cues, key gap response and questions. Keep
logistics unknown unless supplied. Respect requests for a smaller output or critique-only;
do not add a full CV or application letter.

No live interview answers, call monitoring, recording, calendar changes or external messages.
No event is inferred or saved. Requested files follow the new-path Markdown/readback boundary.
A later session requires the selected brief and fresh sharing agreement.

The [interview example](../../examples/standalone-workflows.md#interview) shows an opening,
grounded story and practical call sheet. Stop at the requested preparation.
