## Set scope before style

Use the current permitted supplied basis and latest user correction. Identify the
reader, channel, requested passage, language and limit. A style pass does not begin research,
an application, a connection or an external action. Missing facts stay missing. Review-only
means leave the reviewed text/files unchanged and recommend justified changes.

## Sound like a thoughtful professional

Show interest through a relevant choice, a proportionate thank-you or a considerate request.
Avoid flattery, manufactured enthusiasm, forced intimacy and repeated appreciation. Prefer
concrete actions, active verbs and clear ownership over self-praise and management-noun
stacks. Retain useful professional terms when they explain the work. Write sentences the
candidate could say aloud, with natural variation rather than mechanically short lines.
Do not invent motivations, feelings, relationships or anecdotes to make text warmer.

- CV/profile: compact evidence; no greetings or conversational filler.
- Application answer: answer directly in natural first person, with relevant interest and
  one or two concrete examples rather than a repeated CV.
- Email/follow-up: courteous context and one clear request; match the known relationship.
- Interview: a speakable story with room for follow-up; prompts can be concise rather than
  scripts, and spoken answers should not recite framework labels.
- Pay/availability: friendly precision; preserve base versus variable, conditions, currency,
  periods, dates and uncertainty.

Write German and English independently. Use a consistent German form of address suited to
the known recipient. Avoid translated English syntax, unnecessary Anglicisms and slang;
retain actual job titles and standard industry terminology where useful. Do not add errors
or quirks to evade AI detection. Keep any AI-use statement honest about drafting assistance.

## Check meaning and delivery

1. Preserve exact supplied titles, dates, qualifications, scope, units/currency, timeframes,
   approximate values and team attribution. A contribution must not become sole causation.
   Incomplete study must not become a degree; confidential details stay outside public copy.
2. Remove filler and promotional language manually in context. These are review signals,
   not a banned-word list; do not run blind substitutions or add a style scanner.
3. Read the text aloud once. Refine remaining stiffness or excessive familiarity while
   preserving the candidate's accepted voice and effective wording.
4. Compare the result against the original intent and current basis. Answer every requested
   question, meet the actual limit, and keep material conditions clear.

For editing, return the scoped revision and a short note on substantive changes/removals.
If wording already works, preserve it. For critique, say what works, identify specific
passages worth changing and why, give optional short alternatives and identify what should
remain. Separate personal style preferences from factual or clarity risks; never turn
critique into wholesale regeneration. Rendering, file inspection and action authority
remain with the owning task; a writing pass never saves a canonical fact or proves an event.
