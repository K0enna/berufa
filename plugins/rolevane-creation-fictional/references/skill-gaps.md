# Turn supplied assessments into a skill-gap report

Use this method when the user asks what to learn, which gaps recur, or how to improve
their fit across supplied roles. The invoking Skill's sharing, file and action boundary
still applies. This report is advice, not a new score, verified profile or saved decision.

## Reuse the assessment basis

Use the selected role assessments, requirement excerpts, candidate facts and explicit
feedback already supplied in this conversation. Do not ask for the same material again.
If none is available, explain the minimum needed: one role's requirements and the user's
relevant experience. Do not infer requirements from titles or infer gaps from silence,
generic rejection, missing CV keywords or an unrecorded outcome.

State the included role set and its size. Count an identified vacancy once, even when
the user supplies its posting, assessment and feedback or several copies. Use a supplied
posting identifier/URL or an unambiguous company-role-location association; a repeated
title alone does not establish identity. Keep ambiguous duplicates outside frequency
counts and name the uncertainty. Exclude roles the user has explicitly declined or ruled
out from learning priorities; retain a short exclusion reason. Do not silently exclude a
desired stretch role just because its fit is low. No invented numeric weighting.

For each requirement, preserve its role/source association and distinguish:

- **Supported:** direct supplied experience; no learning gap established.
- **Transferable:** adjacent experience; explain the missing part without erasing it.
- **Confirmed gap:** the user states they lack it, or supplied facts clearly contradict
  the stated requirement. Employer-stated feedback is attributed feedback, not independent
  proof of missing ability.
- **Unknown:** evidence is missing or contradictory. Clarify or locate a suitable example;
  do not prescribe a course as if the skill were absent.

Group genuine synonyms only when they mean the same work. Keep distinct scopes separate,
such as reading a budget versus owning one, coaching staff versus managing managers, or
using a product versus implementing it. Requirements from stale or undated supplied
postings keep their currentness caveat. Counts describe this sample, not the job market.

## Deliver a usable report

Start with the most useful next action, then a compact table:

| Requirement | Status and supplied basis | Relevant roles | Priority | Next action and proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Use distinct role labels and, where identity is clear, `n of N included roles`. Count a
requirement at most once per role. This is requirement frequency, not a claim that the
candidate lacks it in every role. Separate materially different requirements/statuses.
Show unknowns even when no confirmed gaps remain. An empty or unusable sample means
"insufficient basis", not "no gaps"; give the smallest useful clarification.

Set priorities from the user's desired direction, explicit must-have requirements and
recurrence in the included sample. Label judgment as judgment. Keep eligibility gates
(degree, licence), experience/authority gaps, practical constraints and learnable skills
distinct. A short course does not establish a completed degree, management history,
commercial ownership or willingness to travel. Some gaps call for a different target or
an employer question instead of training.

For the top one to three actionable development priorities, suggest a concrete practice
task and observable completion evidence: for example, produce and explain a sample monthly
report using synthetic data. Reuse supplied resources if relevant, labeling their source
and verification limits. Do not browse or invent course links, prices, course availability,
completion times or promised career outcomes. If the user supplies a time budget, fit the
plan to it and distinguish suggested effort from verified duration.

When the user supplies an earlier report, compare only matched roles/requirements and
explicit new evidence. Preserve the old basis and identify changed scope; a different
sample is not proof of learning progress. No automatic tracking, new database, evidence
approval, profile rewrite or pipeline change. Deliver complete text, or requested new
Markdown under the existing profile's file contract.
