# Service handoffs

I would start by reviewing where customer requests lose context between Customer Operations and Product, then agree a simple handoff standard with both teams. At Alder Orbit, I designed a checklist and ran weekly queue reviews while Product addressed two recurring defects. Together, the team reduced median resolution time by approximately 15% in April–June 2025 compared with the previous quarter.

For Cedar Metrics, I would use your emphasis on service reviews to test a similar approach: clear ownership, enough context for the receiving team and a short review of unresolved handoffs. I would first ask which transitions create the most repeat work, so the team can focus on the problem that matters most.
