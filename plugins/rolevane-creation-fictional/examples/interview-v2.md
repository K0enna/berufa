# Interview practice

## Handoff example

The handoff work at Alder Orbit involved our operations team and Product. I designed a short checklist defining the next owner and the context needed for an escalation, and facilitated weekly queue reviews. Product also fixed two recurring defects, so this was a joint effort rather than a result I delivered alone.

Our team's median resolution time was approximately 15% lower in April–June 2025 than in the previous quarter. I would be careful about promising the same improvement elsewhere. What I can bring is the approach: understand where context is lost, agree a practical standard with the people doing the work and review it regularly.

## Degree and implementation gaps

I completed coursework toward a B.Sc. in Business Informatics, but I did not complete the degree. My experience since then has been in customer operations, including direct leadership of five specialists. I saw that a completed degree is listed as mandatory, so I would like to understand whether you consider equivalent professional experience.

I have coordinated escalations and improved handoffs with Product, but I have not owned implementation projects. That is an area I would need to learn. I would first want to understand what ownership the role has and what support is available.

## Questions for the hiring manager

- Which decisions about service standards would I own, and which need another team's approval?
- Where do handoffs with Product create the most repeat work today?
- How much travel is expected, and how do you protect time for direct team coaching?
